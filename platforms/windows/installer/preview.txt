NepType for Windows — typing-pad preview

Windows 10/11, Intel or AMD 64-bit (x64).

Write Roman letters in NepType, choose a spelling, and copy the Nepali output into another app. The engine works offline. This installer does not install, register, or enable a system keyboard, background service, or keyboard hook.

This preview is unsigned. Windows or your browser may show an unknown-publisher or SmartScreen warning. No code-signing or SmartScreen reputation is claimed.

The pad does not save documents. Copy any text you want to keep before closing it.

Installs for your user account without administrator access. Remove it through Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > NepType > Uninstall.
